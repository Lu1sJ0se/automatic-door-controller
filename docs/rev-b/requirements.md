## Functional Requirements

- The controller shall drive a brushed DC motor in both directions.
- The controller shall provide PWM-based speed control.
- The controller shall provide a commanded motor stop condition.
- The controller shall prevent unsafe simultaneous H-bridge conduction.
- The controller shall monitor motor current.
- The controller shall detect an overcurrent condition.
- The controller shall disable the motor power stage following a critical
  overcurrent fault.
- The controller shall provide a fault indication.
